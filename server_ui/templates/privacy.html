{% translate "Privacy policy" %}

{% translate "In general" %}

  • {% blocktranslate trimmed with email=settings.HELP_EMAIL_ADDRESS %}Helios is designed with built-in privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about privacy not covered here, send us an email.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% translate "We collect only the information needed to provide secure, trustworthy elections." %}
  • {% translate "Some information is published as part of the process of auditing elections (details below). Other than that, we do not give away any information we collect." %}

{% translate "For voters" %}

{% translate "About your vote:" %}

  • {% translate "Your vote is encrypted in your browser with the election key before it is sent. The server never sees your vote in the clear." %}
  • {% translate "Your individual vote is never decrypted: the encrypted votes are combined into an encrypted tally (homomorphic encryption), and only the final tally is decrypted." %}
  • {% translate "The trustees of your election could decrypt your individual vote only if all of them colluded. This is why important elections should use several trustees who are independent of each other." %}

{% translate "About other data:" %}

  • {% translate "To vote, you prove your eligibility, depending on the election with a voter ID and password sent to you, or by logging in with an external login service. Helios never sees your password at such a service." %}
  • {% translate "For auditing the election, Helios stores your name, email address and voter ID, and the metadata of your vote: when you voted and how often (only the last vote counts). Election administrators can see this information." %}
  • {% translate "If the election is public, the fact that you voted, the time of your vote and its ballot tracker are published. With voter aliases, your name is replaced by a pseudonym. Email addresses are never published." %}

{% translate "For administrators" %}

  • {% translate "To create and administer elections, you log in with a login service of this installation. Helios keeps only what it needs to recognize you the next time: your name, email address and user ID, and uses your email address to notify you about your elections." %}
